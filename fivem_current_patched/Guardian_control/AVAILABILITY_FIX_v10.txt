Guardian Fire Dispatch v10

Appliance selection now treats every signed-on callsign as dispatchable unless it is committed to another open incident.
Unassign / Release immediately makes the pump selectable again on the same or another ongoing incident.
A callsign only disappears when it is assigned to another open incident or the operator books OFF RUN (which removes it from the active unit board).
MDT radio status such as Home Station, Available Pager, Mobile And Available, etc. no longer incorrectly hides the pump from Control.
