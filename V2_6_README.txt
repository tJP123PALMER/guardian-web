Guardian Operations v2.6 - Turnout + Standby Control

Adds:
- Scrollable MDT turnout slip with sticky ACK/route/clear controls.
- Standby turnout editor before dispatch: postal, map ref, FLAB talkgroup, role, risk and further info.
- Normal incident editor: map ref, talkgroup and special risk.
- Appliance role selected before mobilisation and sent to MDT/turnout.
- Latest turnout brief is saved automatically immediately before mobilisation.
- Stations tab shows incoming temporary standby cover and home appliances away covering elsewhere.
- RETURN HOME now reaches Guardian_control and sends an alerted MDT instruction to the appliance.
- Setting HOME STATION or MOBILE AND AVAILABLE automatically completes/closes the standby incident.
- Real emergency incidents still override standby.

FiveM matched files are under fivem_current_patched. Keep guardian_web/shared/config.lua.
